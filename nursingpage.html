Healthcare & Emergency Nursing

"Clinical frontline precision across surgical theaters, international eldercare standards, and emergency medical response."

Since 2016, I have practiced as a Qualified Nurse Technician, cultivating extensive clinical experience across public and private hospitals, including frontline duty during the height of the Covid-19 pandemic.

In operating theaters, I served as a Certified Surgical Assistant Nurse, ensuring uncompromising patient safety, maintaining sterile operating fields, and managing intraoperative surgical instrumentation across major procedures.

In 2021, I transitioned to international healthcare in Ireland with InisCare and The Marlay Nursing Home, followed by specialized elderly care in Portugal. Recently, I completed formal Emergency Medical Technician (EMT) training aligned with PHECC standards, preparing to volunteer with Irish ambulance rescue operations.

Throughout my healthcare journey, I have remained deeply committed to compassionate, person-centered care while constantly identifying how thoughtful digital tools can reduce caregiver burden and elevate patient outcomes.
