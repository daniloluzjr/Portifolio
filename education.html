🎓 Academic Research & Higher Education

Education & Biomechanical Science

"Investigating human kinetics, advancing biomechanical research, and shaping future healthcare leaders."

My teaching journey commenced in 2006 at the College of Physical Education (YMCA Brazil), driven by a dedication to human movement, athletic pedagogy, and sports science.

Academically, I attained a Postgraduate Specialization in Biomechanics of Physical Activity and Health, followed by a Master of Science (M.Sc.) degree in Biomechanics at Universidade São Judas Tadeu. In 2020, I earned my second higher education degree in Pedagogy (Licenciatura).

As a University Professor, I lectured undergraduate students in Nursing, Physiotherapy, and Physical Education, bridging theoretical biomechanics and systemic anatomy with clinical real-world practice.

This rich pedagogical background empowers me to break down complex multidimensional systems into intuitive, accessible concepts—whether mentoring healthcare professionals or architecting software interfaces.
